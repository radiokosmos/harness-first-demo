Here's the summary. Quick notes before the numbers:

- I queried `raw.support_tickets` directly in `demo.duckdb` (not via a dbt model) — no prod risk since this is the local file.
- The data in the table covers **2026‑10‑01 to 2026‑10‑14 (UTC)**, i.e. roughly **Sep 30 evening – Oct 14 afternoon in Seattle time** — essentially all of this month so far.
- `body` is full of customer PII (emails, phone numbers, IBANs, card numbers, full names), per the team note "never copy PII into marts." I'm extending that same caution here: counts/classification are exact from the raw data, but the quoted examples below have PII redacted (`[email]`, `[phone]`, `[account]`, `[card/ref]`, `[Customer]`) rather than copied verbatim.

## SQL I ran

```sql
select
  case
    when body ilike '%App crashes when I open the chart%' then 'App crashes opening chart'
    when body ilike '%Deposit failed again%' then 'Deposit failures'
    when body ilike '%Verification documents uploaded twice%' then 'Duplicate verification docs / KYC confusion'
    when body ilike '%Please send the refund to%' then 'Refund requests'
    when body ilike '%Change my email to%and the payout account to%' then 'Email + payout account change requests'
    when body ilike '%Spread on DAX was too wide%' then 'Spread complaints (DAX)'
    when body ilike '%Why was my position closed%' then 'Position closed without explanation'
    when body ilike '%My withdrawal is still pending%' then 'Withdrawal pending / stuck'
    when body ilike '%I was charged twice on card%' then 'Double-charged on card'
    when body ilike '%promised a callback%' then 'Missed callback from agent'
    else 'Other / unclassified'
  end as theme,
  count(*) as n
from raw.support_tickets
group by 1
order by n desc;
```

(All 400 tickets classified cleanly — no "other" bucket, the complaint language is fairly templated.)
