Here's what I found in `raw.support_tickets` (DuckDB, queried directly against `demo.duckdb`).

**Window:** all 400 tickets in the table fall in this month — Oct 1–14, 2026 UTC, which is **Sep 30, 6:33 PM – Oct 14, 4:17 PM Seattle time**.

**SQL I ran:**

```sql
select
  case
    when body ilike 'App crashes when I open the chart%'       then '01 App crashes on chart screen'
    when body ilike 'Spread on DAX was too wide yesterday%'     then '02 Wide spreads on DAX'
    when body ilike 'Deposit failed again%'                     then '03 Deposit failures'
    when body ilike 'I was charged twice on card%'              then '04 Double card charges'
    when body ilike 'Verification documents uploaded twice%'    then '05 Verification/KYC stuck'
    when body ilike '%promised a callback%'                     then '06 Missed callback'
    when body ilike '%Why was my position closed?%'             then '07 Position closed without notice'
    when body ilike 'Hi, this is%My withdrawal is still pending%' then '08 Withdrawal pending'
    when body ilike 'Please send the refund to%'                then '09 Refund requests'
    when body ilike 'Change my email to%'                       then '10 Change email/payout account'
    else '99 Other'
  end as theme,
  count(*) as n
from raw.support_tickets
group by 1
order by n desc;
```
